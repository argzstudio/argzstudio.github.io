Local Notes & Sync

Fast notes on your Android phone, editable from your computer's browser over Wi-Fi.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

Edit from your computer

Turn on web access and open your notes in any browser on the same Wi-Fi. Nothing goes through the cloud.

Notes, lists and images

Rich text, to-do lists, pictures and folders, all stored on your phone.

Backup to Google Drive

Optional sync through your own Drive keeps notes safe and moves them to a new phone.

Widgets

Pin a note to your home screen, start a new one in a tap, or switch web access on and off.
